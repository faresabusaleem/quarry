# Quarry brand guide

The source of truth for anything Quarry builds. The tokens it describes live in one file,
[`web/app/tokens.css`](../web/app/tokens.css); the brand files live in
[`web/public/brand/`](../web/public/brand/) and are generated by
[`scripts/brand/build_brand.py`](../scripts/brand/build_brand.py).

Quarry is an analyst that shows its work, so the identity behaves like a good instrument:
calm neutrals, one accent that means something, type built for reading numbers, and nothing
decorative that is not doing a job.

## Name and voice

- **Name:** written `quarry` in the wordmark, "Quarry" in sentences. A quarry is where you
  cut useful stone out of rock; the product cuts answers out of raw data.
- **Voice:** plain, specific, international English. Say what happened and what it means:
  "Ran out of steps", "Demo limit reached", not "Oops!". No exclamation marks, no emoji, no
  "AI-powered", no "magic". Numbers are always attributable to a query.

## Wordmark and lockup

- **Wordmark:** `quarry`, lower case, Instrument Sans weight 600, tracking −0.03em, kerned by
  the font. The files hold outlines (`wordmark.svg`), so the mark renders the same without the
  font. Never retype it.
- **Lockup** (`logo.svg`, `logo-reversed.svg`): icon left, wordmark right. The visible stone is
  1.6 × the wordmark's cap height, centred on its x-height band; the gap is 0.35 × the stone
  height.
- **Clear space:** keep at least half the stone's height free on every side.
- **Minimum sizes:** lockup 20 px tall on screen (the sidebar uses 22 px); icon 16 px, and the
  small variant below 33 px.
- **Colour:** Graphite on light surfaces, Chalk on Graphite. The facet is always Mineral (or
  Mineral light when reversed); it is the only coloured part.

**Do:** use the supplied files; scale proportionally; place on Chalk, White or Graphite.

**Don't:** recolour the body or facet, add a gradient, glow, shadow or outline, rotate the
stone, change the facet lines' weight, put the lockup on a busy photo, or set "quarry" in
another typeface or weight.

## Colour

Eight brand colours. All themes are built from them, and Mineral is the only accent: active
states, focus rings, links, success and the icon facet. It is never decoration.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Chalk | `#F4F4F1` | Page background, sidebar |
| Limestone | `#E6E6E1` | Hover and selected surfaces, subtle fills |
| Border | `#E2E2DC` | Borders and dividers |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Raised surfaces: composer, cards, panels |
| Graphite | `#16181B` | Main text, primary buttons, icon body |
| Graphite muted | `#5C616A` | Secondary text |
| Mineral | `#0F7A55` | The one accent |
| Mineral light | `#4FC795` | The accent on dark backgrounds |

**Derived roles** (`tokens.css`), light / dark:

- Surfaces: `--bg` Chalk / `#171819`, `--surface` White / `#1E1F21`, `--fill` Limestone /
  `#28292B`, `--border` `#E2E2DC` / `#2D2F32`.
- Text: `--text` Graphite / Chalk, `--text-2` Graphite muted / `#A8ABB0`, `--text-3`
  `#6A6F78` / `#8E9196` (captions and placeholders, never on `--fill`).
- Accent: `--accent` Mineral / Mineral light, `--accent-strong` `#0B6646` / `#6FD6AA` for
  accent text on fills, `--accent-subtle` `#E4F1EA` / `#1B2F26` behind cited numbers and
  selected items.
- States: `--danger` `#B42318` / `#F28B8B`, `--warn` `#8A5A00` / `#E3B04B`, each with a subtle
  background and border.

**Dark mode** uses graphite surfaces, never pure black and never blue-tinted (blue never
exceeds red by more than 3 in any surface), Chalk text and the Mineral light accent. It
follows the system setting, and a toggle saves an explicit choice.

## Typography

- **Instrument Sans** for all interface text and headings. **JetBrains Mono** for SQL, code,
  numbers in tables, query ids (`q1`) and trace data. Both are loaded with `next/font`:
  self-hosted, Latin subset, size-adjusted fallbacks, no layout shift.
- Headings use tracking −0.02 to −0.035em; body text uses normal tracking. Numbers in tables
  use tabular figures.

| Token | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--text-display` | 40 → 72 px (fluid) | Landing hero |
| `--text-h1` | 30 → 40 px | Page titles, empty-state heading |
| `--text-h2` | 22 → 28 px | Section titles |
| `--text-h3` | 18 px | Card and panel titles |
| `--text-lead` | 18 px | Introductory paragraphs |
| `--text-body` | 15 px | Interface and answers |
| `--text-small` | 13 px | Secondary interface text |
| `--text-caption` | 12 px | Labels, metadata |
| `--text-mono` | 13 px | Code, ids, table numbers |

## Iconography

[Lucide](https://lucide.dev), one set only: 1.5 px stroke, 16 px in dense UI, 18 px in
navigation, always in a text colour (Mineral only for an active or success state). Icons label
actions next to words; an icon-only button always has an `aria-label`. No sparkles, robots or
magic wands.

## Charts

One palette everywhere, assigned to entities in a fixed order and never cycled: a filter that
removes a series does not repaint the rest.

| Slot | Light | Dark | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Graphite `#16181B` | `#D9D9D3` | The single series of an answer chart; the baseline |
| 2 | Mineral `#0F7A55` | `#34AD7B` | Quarry's agent; the highlighted series |
| 3 | `#9A5C9E` | `#AD79C8` | |
| 4 | `#C08A2E` | `#C2852D` | |
| 5 | `#4A73B0` | `#6A94D4` | |
| 6 | `#B5544A` | `#D9705F` | |

Slots 2 to 6 were checked with a colour-vision validator against the chart surface: every
slot keeps 3:1 contrast, adjacent slots stay at least 10 ΔE apart for deuteranopia and 15 for
normal vision, and lightness and chroma stay inside the readable band. Slot 1 is
deliberately neutral, so a single-series chart stays calm and Mineral keeps its meaning.

Marks are thin: 2 px lines, bars with a 4 px rounded data end and a 2 px surface gap, recessive
grid lines, no dual axes. A legend appears whenever there are two or more series, and every
chart has its data one click away.

## Space, radius, elevation

- **Space:** a 4 px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128.
- **Radius:** 4 (tags), 6 (buttons, inputs), 10 (cards, step cards), 14 (panels), 20
  (composer), full (pills).
- **Elevation:** borders first. Shadows are soft and low (`--shadow-1` to `--shadow-3`): the
  composer and floating panels only. Never coloured, never glowing.
- **Layout:** sidebar 272 px, conversation column 760 px, marketing grid 1200 px with 12
  columns.

## Motion

- Purposeful and short: 120 ms for hover and press, 200 ms for panels and disclosure, 420 ms
  for page-level reveals, eased with `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`.
- Allowed: fading and lifting sections 8 px into place as they scroll in, steps appearing as
  they stream, the facet catching the light once when the landing page loads.
- Not allowed: looping animations other than progress indicators, parallax, bouncing,
  anything that moves while you read.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` sets every duration to 0 and turns off the reveals and
  the facet animation.

## What Quarry never looks like

No gradients, glow, glassmorphism, neon or blurred blobs; no emoji or sparkle icons; no "AI"
badges; no row of three feature cards with icons; no stock illustrations; no invented numbers,
testimonials or customer logos. Every figure on every page comes from a real run in
`evals/results/` or from the live API.
